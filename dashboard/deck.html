How we built it

We rebuilt the real street in a traffic simulator, filled it with cars from the city counts, built the plan an engineer would use, then let a computer search for a better one.

The honest part: our traffic is not exact.

One square per real counted movement. We matched 41 of 96. Traffic engineers aim for about 82.

The counts are from 2018, and 2 of the five intersections were never counted at all. Everything after this inherits that error.

Before any result, the thing we would want a judge to push on. Our traffic has the right shape but it is not exact, and we say so on the page.

The result

+513more trips finished per rush hour
9.1%faster for eastbound drivers
+11.8%more time lost by side street drivers

Our plan finished about 513 more trips in all 10 test hours, and eastbound drivers got through 9 percent faster. The third number is the cost: side street drivers wait longer.
