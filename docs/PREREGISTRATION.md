Committed before any new optimization or evaluation run. We will report the result whichever way it goes.

- Primary endpoint: PM holdout all-requested delay per requested vehicle, `(sum(completed timeLoss + departDelay) + 900 * (unfinished vehicles + cohort teleport events)) / requested vehicles`. Unfinished includes running and never-inserted vehicles; the charge is a convention, not measured delay.
- Constraint: side-origin all-requested delay, computed with the same numerator and origin-specific denominator, must be at most 5% above the paired textbook plan. Enforce the constraint on the multi-seed mean during search and selection; report the final paired mean constraint check for every scenario/window.
- Secondary endpoints: completed-only time loss, EB/WB corridor travel time, unfinished vehicles, and teleports. Also report side completed-only time loss and insertion delay.
- Sensitivity: the primary formula with charges of 0, 900, and 1800 seconds applied to both unfinished vehicles and teleport events.
- Training: 200 trials on common random seeds 0–2, including the exact textbook plan as trial 0; reject candidates with more than baseline + 2 teleports on any paired seed. Rescore the top 10 on seeds 3–12 and select the lowest feasible mean primary score; retain textbook as a feasible fallback. No tuning on final test seeds.
- Final test: brand-new seeds 13–32 (20 seeds), unused by the existing artifacts and audits, comparing textbook, frozen current v1, and v2 on PM holdout, continuous full PM, and estimated night. Preserve existing routes, warmup, drain, phase order and clearances. PM full and holdout overlap and are not independent replications.
- Inference: one-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, alternative candidate < textbook, paired by seed. The confirmatory comparison is v2 versus textbook on the primary PM holdout endpoint, nominal alpha .05; v1 comparisons, full PM, night, sensitivities and secondary endpoints are descriptive/unadjusted. All-zero paired differences give p=1. Report constraint success independently of significance.
- Caveats: fixed fitted demand tests simulation randomness and later queues, not unseen measured traffic; historical 2018 counts, three estimated quarter-hour profiles, imperfect count fit, and teleports limit field applicability.

## Optimization v5 preregistration — 2026-10-30

Committed alone before any v5 code change or run. We will report the result whichever way it goes, including a textbook or v4 selection and failed constraints.

- Demand/network: branch `vta-2024` at `bed0568`; same routes, network, warmup, 1800 s drain, phase order and clearances as v4. No calibration or presentation changes.
- Primary endpoint and hard constraints: unchanged from v4 — PM-holdout all-requested delay with 900 s charge per unfinished vehicle and cohort teleport event; paired mean side-origin all-requested delay, EB and WB corridor travel each <=105% of textbook, every paired seed <=textbook+2 cohort teleport events. Applied separately to training, selection and each final scenario/window.
- One policy, two plans: rush-hour plan in PM, night plan at night. The switch between them is not simulated; each scenario runs one plan for its whole window.
- Rush-hour search: v4 space unchanged (shared cycle 80–180 s, phase-specific max-green multipliers 0.6–1.4, per-signal offset fraction [0,1), passage 2–4 s, mainline minimum 7–30 s capped below max green). Enqueue exact textbook, frozen v3 and frozen v4 parameters as trials 0–2. Train on seeds 0–4, fit hour only. Measured before writing this: on AC power, 3 workers (the code's AC limit), a PM simulation takes about 3.7 s with three in parallel (about 4.5 s alone). Trial count: 600 (about 7.5 s per trial on 3 workers, about 75 min of search; at a pessimistic 6.5 s per simulation it still fits the existing 6500 s worker timeout). With baselines and selection, total wall time is expected at 1.5–2 h.
- Night search: separate study on the existing estimated night demand, whole 23:00–24:00 window, same seeds 0–4 and 5–12, same objective formula and same four constraints against the night textbook plan. Same space except the cycle range is 66.9–180 s (66.9 s is the night textbook cycle and the shortest cycle every signal can hold its bounded greens and clearances). Enqueue exact night textbook as trial 0 (fallback). 200 trials, same 3 workers.
- Selection (both plans): rescore the ten lowest training-feasible trials plus every enqueued trial on seeds 5–12. Feasible means all constraints pass on both cohorts. Movement-group guard: for each finalist, report the worst origin-group change, the largest increase over textbook in selection-cohort mean all-requested delay (900 s charge) among origin groups, where an origin group is the first modeled approach edge of a trip (7 side streets, 4 Stevens Creek entry edges). Tiebreak: among feasible finalists whose selection-cohort mean primary is within 1% of the lowest feasible one, choose the smallest worst origin-group change. Textbook remains the feasible fallback.
- v4 fallback (rush hour only): if the selected trial is not v4 and does not have a strictly lower mean primary than frozen v4 on both seeds 0–4 and seeds 5–12, and v4 is feasible on both, v4's parameters become the rush-hour plan, and we say so.
- Final test: brand-new seeds 73–102 (30 paired seeds, absent from every tracked artifact), used once with frozen settings. PM holdout, continuous full PM and night; compare textbook (`actuated`), frozen v4 (`optuna_v4`) and the v5 policy (`optuna_v5`: rush-hour plan in PM, night plan at night). Full PM and holdout overlap.
- Inference: one-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank, method auto, zero_method wilcox, all-zero differences p=1. Confirmatory 1: v5 < textbook, PM-holdout primary, alpha .05. Confirmatory 2 (night side-street rule, non-inferiority): paired differences v5 side delay − 1.05 × textbook side delay < 0 at night, alpha .05. Fixed sequence: confirmatory 2 counts as a confirmed result only if confirmatory 1 succeeds; both are reported regardless. Everything else, including v4 comparisons, plain v5 < textbook night side test, per-origin results and 0/1800 s sensitivities, is descriptive.
